## 常见问题

**删掉 App 或换手机后，进度怎么找回？**
用同一个 Apple 账号登录 iCloud，重新安装 Pet Kokoni，进度会从 iCloud 自动恢复。没有登录 iCloud 时，进度只保存在这台设备上。

**怎么恢复购买？**
在 Pet Kokoni 的付款页或"设置 → 订阅"里点"恢复购买"。

**怎么取消订阅？**
在 iOS 的"设置 → [你的名字] → 订阅"里找到 Pet Kokoni，取消自动续订。

**深度专注为什么没有屏蔽其他 App？**
深度专注需要"屏幕使用时间"权限。如果之前拒绝过，在 iOS 的"设置 → 屏幕使用时间"里允许 Kokoni 后再试。

**怎么删除我的数据？**
删除 App 会删除设备上的全部数据。iCloud 里的备份在"设置 → [你的名字] → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间 → Kokoni"里删除。

## FAQ

**How do I get my progress back after deleting the app or changing phones?**
Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account and reinstall Pet Kokoni; your progress comes back from iCloud automatically. Without iCloud, progress is kept only on that device.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Tap "Restore purchases" on the purchase page or under Settings → Subscription in Pet Kokoni.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
In iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, find Pet Kokoni and cancel auto-renewal.

**Why didn't Deep Focus block other apps?**
Deep Focus needs Screen Time permission. If you declined it before, allow Kokoni under iOS Settings → Screen Time and try again.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app deletes everything on the device. Delete the iCloud backup under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Kokoni.
