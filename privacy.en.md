# Pet Kokoni Privacy Policy

Version 3 · Effective date: 27 October 2026

Pet Kokoni (shown as Kokoni on your device) is developed and operated by Dewei Meng, an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what information Pet Kokoni handles, where it is kept, and how you can manage it.

## 1. Summary

- Pet Kokoni needs no account and no sign-in.
- Pet Kokoni has no server of its own. What you create in the app stays on your device and in your own backup: on iPhone, your iCloud (if iCloud is on); on Android, your Google account (if the system's automatic backup is on). We cannot see it or obtain it.
- Pet Kokoni has no ads, uses no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you, and does not sell or share any of your information.

## 2. Information kept on your device

To provide the timer, the companion pet, growth and collecting, Pet Kokoni keeps the following on your device:

- Focus and rest records: start and end times, length, focus mode and tag, and the pet that kept you company;
- Paw coin, mystery box and pet unlock records;
- The notes you write down;
- Settings: focus length, tag names, pet names, sound and haptics options;
- The state of your in-app purchases (in versions that offer them).

This information is used only to show your progress and to provide these features in the app. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

## 3. Backup

Pet Kokoni is backed up in one of two ways, depending on the device, as described below.

iPhone: if your device is signed in to iCloud, Pet Kokoni backs up the records, notes and settings listed in section 2 to your own iCloud private database (Apple's CloudKit), so that your progress comes back after you reinstall the app or move to a new device.

- The backup is kept in your iCloud account by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.
- To tell whether the device has switched iCloud accounts, Pet Kokoni keeps an iCloud user identifier provided by Apple on your device. It stays on your device and contains no name, email address or similar information.
- To stop the backup, turn off Kokoni under Settings → [your name] → iCloud. To delete the backup, find Kokoni under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete its data.

Android: if the system's automatic backup is on for your device, Android backs up the records, notes and settings listed in section 2 to your own Google account, so that your progress comes back after you reinstall the app or move to a new device.

- The backup is kept in your Google account by Google under Google's privacy policy. We cannot access it.
- The backup contains only the records, notes and settings listed in section 2, and none of the marker files that belong to this device alone.
- To stop the backup, turn it off in Android's system settings, usually under Settings → Google → Backup; the path may differ slightly between device makers. To delete the backup, delete this device's backup in the backup management in the same place; it contains Pet Kokoni's data.

## 4. System permissions

Pet Kokoni asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, or turn it off later in your system settings (on iPhone in Settings; on Android under Settings → Apps → Kokoni, which may differ slightly between device makers); only that feature is affected.

| Permission | Used for | If declined |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Telling you when a focus or rest session ends | No reminder at the end of a session |
| Microphone and speech recognition | Writing notes by voice. Recognition happens on your device; no recording is uploaded or kept | Notes can still be typed |
| Photos (add only) | Saving gift pictures and videos to your library. Pet Kokoni does not read your library | Gifts cannot be saved, but can still be shared |
| Screen Time (iPhone) | During a Deep Focus session, blocking the apps you choose. Your choice of apps stays on your device and we cannot see it | Deep Focus runs as a normal session |
| Usage access (Android) | During a Deep Focus session, telling which app is open so that the apps you have not allowed can be blocked. This is decided on your device; which apps you use is not recorded or uploaded | Deep Focus runs as a normal session |
| Display over other apps (Android) | During a Deep Focus session, showing your pet over a blocked app | Deep Focus runs as a normal session |
| Battery optimisation (Android, optional) | Keeping a session running in the background without the system stopping it | A session may be stopped by the system in the background |

When you choose the apps allowed during Deep Focus on Android, Pet Kokoni reads the list of apps that can be opened from your home screen (names and icons) on your device, only to let you choose. Your choice stays on your device and we cannot see it.

The countdown on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island (Live Activity) is shown on your device and needs no separate permission; you can turn it off in iOS Settings.

## 5. Purchases

In versions that offer in-app purchases, payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, Apple Account or contact information. Pet Kokoni reads the state of your purchases and trial from the App Store on your device to decide which features to open.

## 6. Exporting and sharing

When you save or share a gift picture or video, the file carries a label stating that it was generated by AI (the software name Pet Kokoni and the gift's number). The file contains no personal information. Once shared to another app, that app's rules apply.

## 7. AI-generated content

The pets' pictures and videos and the gift pictures in the app are generated by AI models. They were generated during development; using Pet Kokoni sends nothing of yours to any AI model.

## 8. Your choices

- View and correct: your records, notes and settings can be viewed in the app; settings and notes can be changed or crossed out at any time.
- Delete: deleting the app deletes everything on your device; delete the iCloud or Google account backup as described in section 3.
- Withdraw consent: Pet Kokoni can be used only after you agree to this policy. You can withdraw by stopping using Pet Kokoni and deleting it.
- Account deletion: Pet Kokoni has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete.

If you have questions about any of this, contact us as described in section 11.

## 9. Children

Pet Kokoni is not designed for children under 14. Minors should use it with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. Pet Kokoni collects no one's personal information; a minor's data likewise stays only on their device and in their iCloud or Google account backup.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we raise the version number and update the effective date. If a change needs your agreement again, Pet Kokoni asks you to read and agree to it the next time you open the app.
