# Kokoni Privacy Policy

Version 1 · Effective date: 20 October 2026

Kokoni (Pet Kokoni on the App Store) is developed and operated by Dewei Meng, an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what information Kokoni handles, where it is kept, and how you can manage it.

## 1. Summary

- Kokoni needs no account and no sign-in.
- Kokoni has no server of its own. What you create in the app stays on your device and in your own iCloud (if iCloud is on). We cannot see it or obtain it.
- Kokoni has no ads, uses no analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you, and does not sell or share any of your information.

## 2. Information kept on your device

To provide the timer, the companion pet, growth and collecting, Kokoni keeps the following on your device:

- Focus and rest records: start and end times, length, focus mode and tag, and the pet that kept you company;
- Paw coin, mystery box and pet unlock records;
- The notes you write down;
- Settings: focus length, tag names, pet names, sound and haptics options;
- The state of your in-app purchases (in versions that offer them).

This information is used only to show your progress and to provide these features in the app. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

## 3. iCloud backup

If your device is signed in to iCloud, Kokoni backs up the records, notes and settings listed in section 2 to your own iCloud private database (Apple's CloudKit), so that your progress comes back after you reinstall the app or move to a new device.

- The backup is kept in your iCloud account by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We cannot access it.
- To tell whether the device has switched iCloud accounts, Kokoni keeps an iCloud user identifier provided by Apple on your device. It stays on your device and contains no name, email address or similar information.
- To stop the backup, turn off Kokoni under Settings → [your name] → iCloud. To delete the backup, find Kokoni under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete its data.

## 4. System permissions

Kokoni asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can decline, or turn it off later in iOS Settings; only that feature is affected.

| Permission | Used for | If declined |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Telling you when a focus or rest session ends | No reminder at the end of a session |
| Microphone and speech recognition | Writing notes by voice. Recognition happens on your device; no recording is uploaded or kept | Notes can still be typed |
| Photos (add only) | Saving gift pictures and videos to your library. Kokoni does not read your library | Gifts cannot be saved, but can still be shared |
| Screen Time | During a Deep Focus session, blocking the apps you choose. Your choice of apps stays on your device and we cannot see it | Deep Focus runs as a normal session |

The countdown on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island (Live Activity) is shown on your device and needs no separate permission; you can turn it off in iOS Settings.

## 5. Purchases

In versions that offer in-app purchases, payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, Apple Account or contact information. Kokoni reads the state of your purchases and trial from the App Store on your device to decide which features to open.

## 6. Exporting and sharing

When you save or share a gift picture or video, the file carries a label stating that it was generated by AI (the software name Kokoni and the gift's number). The file contains no personal information. Once shared to another app, that app's rules apply.

## 7. AI-generated content

The pets' pictures and videos and the gift pictures in the app are generated by AI models. They were generated during development; using Kokoni sends nothing of yours to any AI model.

## 8. Your choices

- View and correct: your records, notes and settings can be viewed in the app; settings and notes can be changed or crossed out at any time.
- Delete: deleting the app deletes everything on your device; delete the iCloud backup as described in section 3.
- Withdraw consent: Kokoni can be used only after you agree to this policy. You can withdraw by stopping using Kokoni and deleting it.
- Account deletion: Kokoni has no accounts, so there is nothing to delete.

If you have questions about any of this, contact us as described in section 11.

## 9. Children

Kokoni is not designed for children under 14. Minors should use it with the consent and guidance of a parent or guardian. Kokoni collects no one's personal information; a minor's data likewise stays only on their device and in their iCloud.

## 10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we raise the version number and update the effective date. If a change needs your agreement again, Kokoni asks you to read and agree to it the next time you open the app.
